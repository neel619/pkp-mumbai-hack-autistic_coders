About CitizenLaw

Bringing justice and awareness closer to every Indian citizen.

Overview

CitizenLaw is a digital public initiative under the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India, dedicated to enhancing legal literacy across the nation. It serves as a bridge between citizens and legal institutions, simplifying legal information and promoting awareness about constitutional rights and duties.

The platform provides information about key laws, rights, duties, government legal aid schemes, and online services. It aims to empower people with knowledge so that justice is accessible, affordable, and transparent for all.

Objectives

⚖️ Legal Awareness

Educate citizens about their constitutional rights, duties, and access to justice.

📚 Simplified Legal Information

Present legal material in easy-to-understand formats using technology and design.

🧑‍⚖️ Accessibility

Ensure that citizens in rural and urban areas can access free legal aid easily.

🤝 Collaboration

Work alongside State Legal Services Authorities, NGOs, and academic institutions.

Vision & Mission

The vision of CitizenLaw is to foster a legally informed society where every citizen understands their rights, obligations, and legal remedies. Through a blend of technology and governance, it strives to build a transparent and responsive justice ecosystem.

The mission is to integrate digital platforms with legal literacy, ensuring that “Justice for All” is not just a constitutional promise but a lived reality.
